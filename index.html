Research Projects

Infrared Detector Based on Colloidal Quantum Dots

Report

This project aims to develop an alternative infrared detector based on colloidal quantum dots (CQDs) made of mercury telluride (HgTe). These semiconductor nanoparticles have a remarkable property: their absorption wavelength can be adjusted simply by controlling their size during synthesis. The larger the dots, the more they absorb low-energy photons, and therefore longer wavelengths. This flexibility paves the way for infrared detectors that could be less expensive and easier to manufacture than conventional technologies.

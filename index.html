This project demonstrates that when future information is unavailable and decisions cannot be reversed, optimal decision-making shifts from exact optimization toward strategies that balance learning and action. Remarkably, simple threshold-based rules can achieve provably optimal or near-optimal performance, highlighting the power of principled heuristics in uncertain environments.

Infrared Detector Based on Colloidal Quantum Dots

Project Repository / Report

This project focuses on the design and development of an infrared detector based on colloidal quantum dots. The scientific and technical objectives of the project are outlined below.

  • Synthesize colloidal quantum dots and characterize them optically and structurally
  • Fabricate a wavelength-selective photoconductive device operating around λ ≈ 5 μm
  • Fully characterize the device and achieve a target figure of merit (D* > 10⁸ Jones at low temperature)
  • Improve device efficiency by reducing noise through cryogenic cooling
  • Integrate the developed detector into a complete optical system
